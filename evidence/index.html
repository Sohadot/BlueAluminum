Evidence policy

Claims need coordinates.

A claim is useful only when its subject, system, boundary, source, and uncertainty can be located.

Five principles

Relevance.
Material truth.
System view.
Context.
Transparency.

No inherited virtue

Aluminum’s durability, low density, formability, conductivity, or recyclability can be relevant. None makes every application sustainable by default.

No universal performance

Alloy, temper, joining, exposure, design, inspection, and maintenance change outcomes. Evidence must match the actual system.

No boundary hiding

Operational benefits cannot silently erase production impacts; recycled content cannot silently erase losses, coatings, or unavailable recovery pathways.

No certification by association

BlueAluminum.com classifies and explains. It does not audit facilities, certify products, or confer environmental labels.
