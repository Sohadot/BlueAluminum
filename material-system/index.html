Lifecycle view

A system, not a specimen.

Material capability is created and lost across a chain of decisions. The category follows that chain.

Input

Primary and recycled content, energy context, provenance, alloy availability.

Transformation

Alloying, extrusion, rolling, casting, joining, coating and fabrication yield.

Service system

Loads, exposure, maintenance, inspection, repair and operational life.

Next use

Identification, disassembly, sorting, remelting, retention and verified loss.

Decision layers

Four truths must coexist.

Material truth

Alloy, temper, geometry, joining, coating, and actual properties.

System truth

What the component enables, what failure means, and what alternatives change.

Environmental truth

Production route, energy, longevity, maintenance, losses, and recovery—within a declared boundary.

Governance truth

Who made the claim, which evidence supports it, and where uncertainty remains.
