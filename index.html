Category boundary

Use is not enough.
Context must be proven.

Blue Aluminum names aluminum whose function, engineering, and lifecycle are materially connected to a blue-economy system. It does not turn every marine-adjacent product into a sustainability claim.

Included

Products and systems integral to maritime mobility, offshore energy, coastal infrastructure, aquaculture, ocean systems, ports, or marine circularity.

Conditional

Applications with partial ocean relevance or unresolved performance, sourcing, traceability, service-life, or recovery evidence.

Excluded

Generic aluminum with no material blue-economy function, or claims based only on color, location, marketing language, or proximity to water.
